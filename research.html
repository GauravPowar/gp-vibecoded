CURRENT FOCUS

Molecular Toxicology & Hepatology

Dual Role of Apigenin in Liver Health

Investigating the dose-dependent therapeutic window of the plant flavone Apigenin. At physiological concentrations, it triggers cellular hepatoprotective defenses through Nrf2/ARE pathways; however, supraphysiologic doses elicit severe hepatotoxicity via mitochondrial dysfunction, reactive oxygen species accumulation, and MAPK/NF-κB signaling cascades.

Phytochemistry Molecular Toxicology MAPK Signaling Cell Viability
LONG-TERM GOAL

Gene Editing & Metabolic Disorders

CRISPR-Cas9 Systems in Metabolic Disorders

Modeling complex cellular pathways governing insulin resistance in hereditary Type-2 Diabetes. The project leverages targeted CRISPR-Cas9 knock-ins and knock-outs to eludicate precise genetic corrections at insulin receptor substrates, studying metabolic correction efficiency and minimizing off-target genomic cleavage.
